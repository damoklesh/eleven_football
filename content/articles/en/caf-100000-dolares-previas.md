For many clubs, a continental preliminary round is more than a match: it is a budget-defining trip. Flights, hotels and bonuses arrive before the tournament’s larger revenues do.

The competition’s growth should therefore be measured not only by spectacle, but by whether it protects the clubs taking the financial risk to enter it.
