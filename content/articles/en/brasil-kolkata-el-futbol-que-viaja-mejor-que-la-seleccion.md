The distance between Brazil and Kolkata is enormous, yet the game creates a familiar language. Shirts, songs and improvised pitches keep the idea of Brazil alive beyond results.

That worldwide attachment is an asset, but it also raises the standard: the national team is asked to reconnect its performance with the imagination it still inspires.
